01 — THE ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT

THE ORIGINAL PROJECT

A landmark
above the city.

53 West 53 is a residential skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan, adjoining the Museum of Modern Art. Its striking silhouette was conceived by architect Jean Nouvel, with interiors by Thierry Despont.

That established architectural identity forms the reference point for the independent media-design study presented here.
